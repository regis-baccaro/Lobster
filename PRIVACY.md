# Lobster privacy policy

*Last updated: October 25, 2026 · Freia tech*

Lobster converts and edits files **on your computer**. It does not collect, store or send any
personal data.

- **No uploads.** Files you open with Lobster are processed locally and never leave your device.
- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.** Lobster contains no telemetry.
- **Local data only.** Lobster keeps its settings and a diagnostic log (`lobster.log`) in your
  user profile. They stay on your device and you can delete them at any time.
- **Network use.** Lobster only connects to the internet when you ask it to install an optional
  engine (for example ffmpeg through winget, or a package manager on Linux and macOS). Those tools
  are run by your system and follow their own privacy policies.
- **Permissions.** Lobster watches mouse and modifier keys only to detect the Shift + drag
  gesture; nothing is recorded or transmitted.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/regis-baccaro/Lobster/issues>.
